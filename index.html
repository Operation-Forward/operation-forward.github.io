What we do

Your path forward

Three steps, each free, from the basics to proof you can do the job.

  1. 1

    Bridge courses

    Self-paced courses that build the foundation you need before a bootcamp. If you don't yet meet a certification's starting requirements, a bridge course gets you there.

  2. 2

    Certification bootcamps

    Intensive courses for industry certifications from providers such as CompTIA, (ISC)², AWS and Azure, taught by professionals who hold the same certification or higher. Mentoring, study resources and practice exams included.

  3. 3

    Project Hub

    Work in a student team on a real technical project for a local nonprofit, with weekly check-ins and monthly presentations to leadership and mentors. Verifiable experience for your resume, and real help for the community.

Our story

From a garage home lab to a career forge

Operation Forward was founded by Daniel Farrell, a 20-year veteran of the United States Air Force. After two decades as an Emergency Manager, he prepared for a move into tech with a Master's degree in Cybersecurity, and found a gap: bootcamps and even advanced degrees taught the theory, but not the hands-on confidence to secure a real network.

He closed that gap in a home lab in his garage, a place to fail, fix and learn without consequence, and then started helping other transitioning veterans do the same. The mission took shape during a SkillBridge internship in Hawaii, where he built the tools that turn students into practitioners.

Today Operation Forward is more than a school. It is a career forge that removes the financial barrier and provides the lab environments today's jobs require.

Our vision: the Pikes Peak region as a hub of accessible tech talent and community-driven innovation.

Community-led innovation

Founded by veterans, for veterans and their neighbors: career success and mentorship come first.
