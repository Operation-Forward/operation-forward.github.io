Operation Forward is a charity that removes the cost barrier to a career in technology, so talent, not bank accounts, decides who gets one.

Our mission

Skills that move people forward

To empower adults in the Pikes Peak region, especially veterans and career-changers, with free, career-focused technology training.

We build a stronger local workforce by providing free certification bootcamps and collaborative projects that solve real community needs, while our students build hands-on skills and professional resumes.

What we provide

A free path from the basics to the job

Bridge courses

Self-paced courses that build the foundation a student needs before starting a certification bootcamp.

Certification bootcamps

Free courses for industry certifications such as CompTIA, (ISC)², AWS and Azure, taught by working professionals, with exam vouchers at no cost for students who complete the coursework.

Project Hub

Student teams build real technical projects for local nonprofits, with mentors, weekly check-ins and experience they can put on a resume.

Hands-on labs

Realistic lab environments where students practice, fail, fix and learn without consequence, the way the job is really done.

Our goals

What we want to accomplish

  • No cost barrier. Tuition, workspace and labs free for every student.
  • Confidence, not just theory. Graduates who have done the work in realistic environments, not only read about it.
  • Credentials and experience. Recognized certifications and verifiable project work that employers value.
  • Help for local nonprofits. Real technical projects delivered to organizations in our community.
  • A stronger region. The Pikes Peak region as a hub of accessible tech talent and community-driven innovation.
